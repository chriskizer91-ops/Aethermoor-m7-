# The story on the map

M7's story kept as it is, its places set on Chris's atlas of Aethermoor (the land the 3D continent is built from).
A proposal of November 20, for Chris to confirm. Published: https://claude.ai/artifact/XTicYjY9EcZ4V54cjSzJcZ

- `places.json`: the 32 places: where each stands (atlas pixels, 4608 x 3072), what happens there (M7's story),
  which finished 3D place can be it (Coven's zone 1 is in `world3d/`; Envoi's others are in
  `envoi-on-the-longest-night` `moon-faire-witches/world3d/places/`), what changed from M7, and the roads.
- `build_story_map.py` + `story-map.tpl.html` → `aethermoor-story-map.html`. `atlas-2048.webp`: Chris's atlas,
  shrunk (from `envoi-on-the-longest-night` `reference/art/world-map/day/Aethermoor-Nine-Region-Atlas.webp`).

## Why it fits

M7's own map has the Keep in the middle, the Wilds to the west and north-west, the Gloomfen south-west, the Ironspire
north and north-east, the Sunscorch east and south-east. The atlas has the same: the Keep on an island in an inner sea,
western forests, southern wetlands (painted for "Bogmire and Willowmurk", by the atlas's own region name), north-east
peaks, the eastern desert. M7 gives the Keep four roads out (the Hearth Road, the Old Bridge, the Sunward Road, the
Blackwater Causeway): on the island they become a boat west, a bridge north, a crossing east and a causeway south.
The story's order stays M7's: the Keep, Act I in the Wilds (two Brands), Act II's three regions in any order (six
Brands), Act III under the Keep.

## What changes (Chris's calls are listed on the page)

- The Hearth Road runs west, not north. Fawnrest and the Highfold no longer join.
- Frostmere comes earlier, on the road up to the peaks.
- Places move to where their ground is (Eldergrove south into the forest's heart, the Thornway south-east of
  Thornhollow); finished 3D places from Envoi and Coven serve as fourteen of the 32 under M7's names.
