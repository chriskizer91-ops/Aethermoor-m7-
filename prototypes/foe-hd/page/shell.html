How the full-detail one is made. A sculpt of triangles is built in code from measurements of Chris's sheet: his coat is about 1,500 big leaf-feather tufts sculpted into one surface, with the thorn ridge, the crown, the vines, tusks and claws. Blender bakes its surface, its shading and its colour into three pictures that are wrapped on a light version of about 50,000 triangles, so many of him can be on screen at once. He has 19 bones for his moves.

The sculpt itself (about a million triangles) has a page of its own for a laptop, beside the light version, so you can see how much of it the bake keeps. It is saved in the game's files as a model of its own.
