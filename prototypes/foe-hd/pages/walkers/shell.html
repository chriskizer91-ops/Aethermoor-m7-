How each one is made. A shape model of about 100,000 triangles is built in code from measurements of the sheet and checked against its outlines from the front, side and back. Then the detail goes on, up to a million triangles: real plates with raised gold rims and rivets, roots twisted from strands, moss in cushions of lumps, ridged bark. No fuzz of tiny hairs. Blender bakes that sculpt's surface, its shading, its colour and which parts are metal into four pictures, wrapped on a light model a phone can draw many of. Each also has a phone version, lighter still (fewer triangles, smaller pictures): phones show it first, and the "Phone version" button switches between the two. It has bones, and its moves are the ones it uses in M7's fights.
