How each one is made. A sculpt is built in code from measurements of Chris's sheet: first the shape (about 100,000 triangles), checked against the sheet's outline from the front, side and back; then the detail, up to 600,000 to a million triangles. Fur is sculpted as big locks that overlap, not as tiny hairs; leaves, thorns and claws are real shapes. Blender bakes the sculpt's surface, its shading and its colour into pictures wrapped on a light model that can share the screen with a whole fight, and the light model has bones for its moves.

The pack. For the foes that come in packs, five of them walk round the four heroes; the number above is this device's frame rate (30 or more is smooth).
