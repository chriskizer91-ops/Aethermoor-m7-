The crowd test. Six of walking round the four heroes, all in full detail. The number above is this device's frame rate; 30 or more is smooth, 60 is very smooth.

How the full-detail one is made. A sculpt of triangles is built in code from measurements of Chris's sheet, its surface sculpted as real shapes (tufts, plates, folds), not painted on. Blender bakes its surface, its shading and its colour into three pictures wrapped on a light model of triangles, so many can be on screen at once. It has bones for its moves.

The sculpt itself has a page of its own for a laptop, beside the light model, so you can see how much of it the bake keeps. It is saved in the game's files as a model of its own.
